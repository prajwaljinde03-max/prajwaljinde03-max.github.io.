README.md

I'm a B.Tech Aerospace Engineering student at MIT ADT University, Pune, focused on Computational Fluid Dynamics, robotics and embedded prototyping. My core research is non-mechanical thrust vector control using fluidic secondary injection, analysed in ANSYS Fluent for next-generation UAV and eVTOL propulsion.

I like closing the loop between simulation and hardware: validating flow-field results numerically, then building the Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi systems that put ideas on a bench. At the IIT Jammu Central Workshop (Designovation Internship Program 2026) I worked on mechanical design, rapid prototyping and precision assembly, and co-invented a patent-pending inflatable cervical support with integrated oxygen delivery for high-altitude emergency operations.

Beyond the lab, I served as lead anchor at an international symposium. I'm looking for research projects, internships and collaborations in propulsion, aerodynamics and aerospace systems.
